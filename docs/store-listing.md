약을 며칠 빠트려도 다그치지 않습니다. 연속 기록 배지도, 재촉 알림도 없습니다. 오늘 약을 먹었는지, 오늘 마음이 어땠는지를 조용히 남기고, 진료일에 그 기록을 한 장으로 들고 가는 것. 이 앱이 하는 일은 그게 전부입니다.

· 기록 없이 지나간 시간대 - 며칠 빠트렸는지 헷갈릴 때, 남은 알약을 다시 세어 기록과 맞춰 볼 수 있습니다. 앱이 짐작하지 않고, 사용자가 날짜별로 답합니다.
· 마음 기록 - 기분 색 하나, 한 줄 메모, 감정 단어, 수면과 꿈, 증상까지. 쓰고 싶은 만큼만 씁니다.

### Description
```
The Janjan is a medication and mood journal made for people taking psychiatric medication.

Miss a few days? It never scolds you. No streaks, no nagging. You quietly note whether you took today's meds and how today felt, then bring it all to your next appointment on a single page. That is all this app does.

■ Free

· Dose log - morning, noon, evening, bedtime, or any time slot you add. Record taken, skipped, or as-needed (emergency) doses.
· Reminders - tap Taken or Skipped right on the lock screen. Notifications are optional; everything works without them.
· Unrecorded time slots - when you lose track of a few days, recount your remaining pills and reconcile them with your records. The app never guesses; you answer day by day.
· Mood journal - one mood color, a one-line note, emotion words, sleep and dreams, symptoms. Write as much or as little as you want.
· Visit report - adherence since your last visit, this month's mood ripples, pills remaining per medication, and questions for your doctor, all on one PDF you can bring to the clinic.
· Prescription log - note your visit date and the meds you picked up, and see the days remaining until your next visit.

■ Pro (subscription)

· Pharmacy bag scan - reads medication names from a photo of the bag. Photos are never saved.
· Find by pill appearance - narrow down a pill by shape, color, and imprint, based on Korean MFDS pill identification data. Results are always shown as guesses.
· Run-out forecast - see ahead of time whether your meds will last until the next visit.
· Dose change comparison - view mood, sleep, and symptom records for the two weeks before and after a dose change, side by side. Interpretation belongs in the consultation room.
· Hide medication names - keep names covered on screen and tap to reveal only when you need to.
· Apple Watch app - log doses, mood, and symptoms right from your wrist.

■ What this app stands for

· No judgment - it never says "well done" or "you missed it." Records state facts only.
· Your records are yours - there is no server. Records live only on your device and in your own iCloud; the developer collects no data.
· No login - start right away without an account. App lock (Face ID / passcode) is available.
· Korean and English supported.

■ Please note

· This app is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Follow your doctor's instructions for diagnosis and prescriptions.
· Medication data quotes public data from the Korean Ministry of Food and Drug Safety, with sources shown.
· Pro is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly; the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial). Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and the subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. You can cancel anytime in your iOS subscription settings.
```
